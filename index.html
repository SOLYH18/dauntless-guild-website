Why Dauntless?

Whether you're fresh off the boat or decked out in +10 reds, there's a home for you here.

⚔️

Massive PvE Community

We don't just claim to be the PvE powerhouse in Insanity — we have the stats to back it up. Hundreds of raids every month across T4, COLO, SR, TT, and ASCND. Raids fire around the clock — there's always a lineup ready for you.

🌱

Newbie-Friendly

New to the game? We've got you. Gear advice, T4 learning runs, and COLO training sessions — you'll catch up faster here than anywhere else. Everyone starts somewhere, and we make sure you start strong.

🎮

Casuals Welcome

No mandatory daily quotas. No guilt trips for missing a raid. Join when you want, chill when you don't. We're a guild that feels like a group chat with benefits — we talk trash, share memes, and actually enjoy each other's company.

🔐

SR Team Recruiting

Our dedicated Secret Room guild is looking for hungry players. Well-geared, competitive, eager to learn strats and run consistent attempts. Enthusiasm over ego — we don't tolerate toxicity. Think you've got what it takes?

🤖

Discord Innovations

Our server isn't just a voice channel and a text room. We've built custom systems — automated raid tracking, class-specific leaderboards, and detailed participation stats — so you always know exactly where you stand. We don't just play the game. We build tools to play it better.

🎉

Community Events

We don't just raid and log off. Our community hosts regular guild events, giveaways, raffles, and achievement challenges to keep things fresh, fun, and social. Boredom simply isn't an option here.

If you want a guild where raids are constant and effort gets rewarded, Dauntless is the place.

🍀 The Clover System

Complete daily quests, earn clovers, build streaks — a little friendly competition never hurt

🎯

Daily Quests

Each day you get a unique quest — bring the requested items and earn 1–10🍀 per turn-in.

📦

Daily Cap

Maximum 20🍀 per day from quests and turn-ins. Spend your extras in the clover shop!

🔥

Streak Bonuses

Keep the streak alive — bonuses unlock at 3, 7, 14, and 25 consecutive days of clover activity.

🏪

Clover Shop

Redeem your hard-earned clovers for exclusive perks — special roles, raid priorities, and more.

📊

Leaderboard

Check your rank with .cloverboard in Discord — daily, monthly, and all-time standings.

⏫

Double Events

Owners can activate 2x clover events for limited windows — double the earnings, double the glory.
